    How each fill gets its sentiment

    • 1 The fill’s time in IST is cut to its date.
    • 2 The fill takes that date’s Fear and Greed class.
    • 3 Fills are grouped by class: count, total and mean PnL, mean size.

    One limit: opening fills book no PnL, so every win rate here is a share of all fills, not of closed trades.

    What counts as a trader win?

    A fill whose closed PnL is above zero; opening fills never count as wins.

    Does greed make traders better?

    The data cannot say why: it shows when these accounts won.

    Where is the trade data file?

    Not in the repository; output/ holds the tables and charts made from it.

    Which exchange is the trade data from?

    Hyperliquid. Each fill has a time, price, size, fee and closed PnL.

    Which files does the analysis script read?

    fear_greed_index.csv and historical_data.csv, placed beside the script.
